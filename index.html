About us

Potential, backed with a place to learn.

At Nexperts, we believe in the power of potential. The Nexperts Grants Program is created to uplift driven individuals by providing them with the resources needed to pursue advanced learning and practical skill development.

Our mission is to support emerging talent—those who are eager to grow, innovate, and make a lasting impact in their fields. Whether you're a student stepping into a new academic chapter or a professional seeking to upskill, this grant is here to back your journey.

How to apply

Six steps, in order.

Fill in the form on this page. The letter is emailed to you from a no-reply address. The document steps after that happen once the email arrives.

1

Obtain the Letter of Enrolment

You must obtain a Letter of Enrolment (Surat Tawaran Kursus) from Nexperts Academy.

2

Fill in the form

Choose your intake and enter your details. Your letter is generated from that form.

3

Prepare your documents

Prepare these with a signature and a cop pengesahan salinan benar / CTC stamp: Identity Card (NRIC) in colour, birth certificate, bank statement for the 3 recent months (front page only), and your SPM certificate only if that is your highest education.

4

Get a CTC stamp

Obtain a CTC (Certified True Copy) stamp on those documents.

5

Sign the letter and send it for checking

Sign the last page of the Letter of Enrolment and send a copy of all pages, together with the CTC documents, by WhatsApp at +60 11-1221 6870 or by email to asma@nexpertsacademy.com or aisyah@nexpertsacademy.com. Do not reply to the offer email. That mailbox is not monitored.

6

Choose Silver Package only

After the documents have been checked, apply for Yayasan Peneraju and select only the Silver Package. Upload the full Letter of Enrolment on the application website.

Course outline

What CEH v13 covers

The official EC-Council syllabus has 20 modules. It moves from foundations and discovery into exploitation, specialised attack surfaces, and defensive countermeasures. AI-assisted ethical hacking runs through the course, rather than sitting in one module.

  • 20 modules
  • 221 hands-on labs
  • 550+ attack techniques
  • 4,000+ hacking and security tools

Phase 1 · Discover

Modules 01–04

  1. 01 Introduction to Ethical HackingInformation security, cyber threats, ethical hacking methodologies, laws, standards, and security controls.
  2. 02 Footprinting and ReconnaissanceOSINT, search engines, websites, email, WHOIS, DNS, social networks, and reconnaissance.
  3. 03 Scanning NetworksHost discovery, ports, services, operating systems, and network scanning.
  4. 04 EnumerationUsers, groups, resources, services, DNS, and protocol enumeration.

Phase 2 · Find the weakness

Modules 05–06

  1. 05 Vulnerability AnalysisIdentify, classify, assess, and prioritise vulnerabilities.
  2. 06 System HackingAuthentication attacks, gaining access, privilege escalation, maintaining access, and attack evidence.

Phase 3 · Understand attacks

Modules 07–12

  1. 07 Malware ThreatsTrojans, viruses, worms, ransomware, rootkits, fileless malware, and malicious payloads.
  2. 08 SniffingPacket capture, traffic analysis, sniffing attacks, and countermeasures.
  3. 09 Social EngineeringPhishing, impersonation, manipulation, and credential-targeting attacks.
  4. 10 Denial-of-ServiceDoS and DDoS attacks, detection, and mitigation.
  5. 11 Session HijackingSession attacks, session-management weaknesses, and countermeasures.
  6. 12 Evading IDS, Firewalls and HoneypotsSecurity monitoring, prevention technologies, evasion concepts, and defensive countermeasures.

Phase 4 · Attack surfaces

Modules 13–17

  1. 13 Hacking Web ServersWeb-server vulnerabilities, attacks, assessment, and hardening.
  2. 14 Hacking Web ApplicationsWeb-application vulnerabilities, attack methodologies, and protection.
  3. 15 SQL InjectionSQL injection concepts, methodologies, detection, and countermeasures.
  4. 16 Hacking Wireless NetworksWi-Fi, authentication, encryption, wireless attacks, and defensive controls.
  5. 17 Hacking Mobile PlatformsMobile vulnerabilities, attack vectors, and security controls.

Phase 5 · Modern infrastructure

Modules 18–19

  1. 18 IoT and OT HackingIoT devices, operational technology, industrial environments, and security threats.
  2. 19 Cloud ComputingAWS, Azure, Google Cloud, IAM, containers, Docker, Kubernetes, serverless, cloud vulnerabilities, and controls.

Phase 6 · Protect information

Module 20

  1. 20 CryptographyEncryption, hashing, symmetric and asymmetric cryptography, PKI, certificates, signatures, and cryptanalysis.
